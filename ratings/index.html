POSITIONAL GRADES,
EXPLAINED.

The A–F grade tells you how naturally a Pokémon’s current profile fits a particular spot on the football pitch. It is a positional fit indicator, not a guarantee of match performance or a universal Pokémon strength ranking.

How to read the grades

AElite fit
BStrong fit
CGood fit
DSituational
ELimited fit
FPoor fit

The grade is based on a blend of underlying Pokémon attributes, interpreted through the demands of the position. The Free Builder deliberately uses broad grade bands rather than publishing individual scores, exact attribute weights, or calculation thresholds.

What matters by position

In this alpha, positions are grouped into four general profiles: goalkeeper, defender, midfielder, and attacker. Similar positions share the same rating family while the balance model continues to evolve.

GK

Goalkeeper

Durability, defensive resilience, and mobility matter most in the current model. A high grade means a strong all-around keeper profile, rather than guaranteed saves.

CB

Centre Back

Resistance to pressure, physical sturdiness, challenges, and recovery pace contribute to defensive fit.

LB · RB · LWB · RWB

Fullbacks and wingbacks

The same broad defensive profile is used in this alpha, balancing defensive strength and mobility. Dedicated wide-defender grades are planned.

CDM · CM

Defensive and central midfield

A balanced profile involving distribution potential, defensive contribution, and pace. These roles currently use the shared midfield model.

LM · RM · CAM

Wide and attacking midfield

The midfield profile is used here too. A dedicated creative-midfield and wide-playmaker calculation is a future refinement.

LW · RW

Wingers

Attacking threat, quickness, and ability to contribute going forward are emphasized. Wingers currently share the broad attacking profile.

ST

Striker

Attacking ability and speed are especially important, with additional emphasis on offensive contribution. The same broad attacking fit is presently used for wingers.

TEAM XI

Your team's grade

Combines the position-fit evaluations of the Pokémon you've selected. It reflects only filled positions until your full eleven is chosen.

What a grade doesn't tell you

Grades do not show exact underlying Pokémon stats, a predicted match score, or how a specific opponent’s tactics will play out. Pokémon type, height and weight appear as scouting information but are not currently part of the Builder's positional-fit formula. Match outcomes also depend on the simulation engine and tactics.

These are provisional alpha grades. As positional roles and the shared match engine are calibrated, grades may change. The exact numerical formula is not published in this guide.
