## Extra-time generation cap

Approved follow-up: cap new extra-time goal/save generation to fit the three-minute rule. Compute one shared incident capacity across both teams from the last regulation incident, the 30-second extra-time opening, and the 119.95-minute endpoint (normally ten incidents). Once capacity is filled, later would-be on-target attempts finish off target before official goal/save outcomes or statistics are recorded. Every attempted shot remains in the ledger. Team/player shots, shots on target, goals, saves and assists reconcile with accepted outcomes. RNG draws are still consumed for the underlying attempt and helper so unrelated shots, passes and tackles retain their deterministic draws. `incidentSpacing` reports capacity and the number of capped attempts; affected shot records carry `spacingLimited: true`.

All three formerly failing seeds complete with ten incidents and one capped attempt apiece. Existing regulation results and stored results are never rewritten. Rare capped extra-time attempts can change the extra-time score and knockout winner compared with uncapped generation; this is the approved rule change. Impossible manually supplied ledgers still raise the scheduler's atomic capacity error. Shootouts remain separate and unchanged.
